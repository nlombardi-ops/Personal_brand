<objective>
Make the CV builder (`app/cv/**`) and the Finance dashboard (`app/dashboard/**`) usable at
iPhone widths (~375–430px) by giving each surface the mobile nav pattern that already works
in `app/community-president/**`, and by unwinding the two forced-desktop split views in the
CV builder.

Purpose: both surfaces currently have zero mobile treatment on their shell. The dashboard
renders a fixed 240px sidebar over a 390px screen with `<main className="ml-60 ...">`; the
CV builder does the same at 224px and then compounds it — `/cv` and `/cv/cover-letter` wrap
the whole page in a hard `1024px` minimum width, so the entire viewport scrolls sideways on
any phone. The page-level content in both surfaces is already responsive; the shells are
what is broken.

Output: eight touched files. No new dependencies, no new routes, no data-model changes, no
new abstractions. This is a structural/visual fix only.

**Explicit non-goals — do not do these even if they look tempting:**
- Do NOT touch anything under `app/community-president/**` or
  `app/components/community/**`. That is the reference pattern, not a target.
- Do NOT merge the CV and Dashboard `layout.tsx` files into a shared layout. Each keeps its
  own independent auth gate; they only share the *structural* nav shape.
- Do NOT cross-pollinate visual styling. CV keeps its own palette (`#0f172a` tile, dark
  active links); Dashboard keeps its `stone-*` / `rounded-lg` convention and emerald tile.
- Do NOT touch `app/dashboard/page.tsx`, `app/dashboard/bills/`, `app/dashboard/insurance/`,
  `app/dashboard/mortgage/`, the recharts components, or
  `app/components/dashboard/BillsTable.tsx`. All already responsive (`sm:`/`lg:` grids,
  `ResponsiveContainer`, an existing `overflow-x-auto` table wrapper). They self-fix once
  the shell stops clipping width.
- Do NOT modify `app/dashboard/login/page.tsx`. It was checked during planning and is
  already fine at 390px (`flex min-h-screen items-center justify-center bg-stone-50 px-4`
  around a `w-full max-w-sm` card), and `DashboardShell` early-returns for that route so the
  sidebar never mounts there.
- Do NOT modify `app/components/cv/CvPreview.tsx`. Its 595px width is deliberate (A4). The
  containment happens at its single call site — confirmed by grep, `/cv/page.tsx` is the
  only consumer.
- Do NOT change the auth gate in `app/cv/layout.tsx`. Its known weak `token !==
  process.env.DASHBOARD_TOKEN` check is a tracked, deliberately-deferred item (see
  `.planning/STATE.md` blockers); hardening it here would smuggle a security change into a
  CSS-shaped commit. Keep the guard clause byte-identical.
</objective>
