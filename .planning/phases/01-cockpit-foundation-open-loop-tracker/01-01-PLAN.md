## Phase Goal (MVP user story)

**As a** president of my comunidad de propietarios, **I want to** capture every open
commitment and incidencia in a private cockpit and see them ranked by urgency the moment I
open it, **so that** I always know what I promised to follow up on and what is waiting on
someone else — without digging through email.

> knows what is pending on open). Nothing was invented. If you want the ROADMAP line itself
> reformatted, run `/gsd mvp-phase 1`.

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Seam:** `OpenLoop.source` gains a `neighbour_form` member and a second entity `Submission`
is introduced where there was previously one president-authored concept.

**Decision: add-alongside, with recorded rationale.** `OpenLoop` stays the primary
president-owned entity and keeps every president-internal field. `Submission` is a separate,
minimal neighbour-intake shape that a *future* phase converts INTO an `OpenLoop` carrying
`source: "neighbour_form"`. We deliberately do NOT promote a general "Item"/"Issue" identity
over both.

**Why:** PLAT-03's entire purpose is that adding the neighbour portal in v2 does not force a
rewrite of the cockpit, and the cheapest guarantee of that is that neighbour-visible data has
its own shape with no president internals on it. A promoted common supertype would re-couple
exactly what PLAT-03 wants decoupled and would tempt fields to migrate upward into the shared
identity.

**Accepted debt:** two shapes describing overlapping subject matter, and a conversion step
that does not exist yet.

**What would force a promote later:** if neighbours ever need to see or track their
submission's *lifecycle* as a first-class object (status transitions, history, notifications),
`Submission` stops being an inbox record and starts needing the same machinery as `OpenLoop` —
that is the trigger to revisit. Not a blocker for Phase 1.
</assumption_delta_decision>
